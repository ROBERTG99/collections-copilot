## What the offline numbers mean

Offline runs use FakeLLM, a deterministic stand-in that reads notes and replies with keyword rules. They measure the code that owns every decision with a side effect (priority score, compliance engine, STOP rule, approval and write-back guards, schema validation and PII redaction) end to end, and they catch regressions in it. They say nothing about how well a real model reads free text; see `docs/evaluation.md`. Open gaps are listed in `docs/KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md`.
